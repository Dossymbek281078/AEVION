## Sending discipline

1. **One email per day, max.** No batch-blasting — each Tier-1 target deserves a fresh sent-from.
2. **Send between 7-9 AM Pacific time** (Tuesday-Thursday) for max open rate.
3. **48-hour follow-up rule:** if no reply, one polite follow-up max. Then stop.
4. **Track replies in a spreadsheet** — date sent, date opened (use trackable links if comfortable, plain text if not), reply status.
5. **Never CC multiple Tier-1 buyers.** Single-target sends only.
6. **If one replies positively** — pause sends to other Tier-1s for 14 days while you process that conversation (avoid being a flake who shops you in parallel).
