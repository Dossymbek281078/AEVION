## LinkedIn — post

**I shipped an AI investment analyst that gives you a memo, not a vibe.**

Most "AI for investors" tools output a fuzzy score you can't interrogate. I wanted the opposite: something a serious investor could actually defend in an IC meeting.

QVenture takes a plain description of any company, in any sector, and returns three things in about 30 seconds:

→ **A transparent 0–100 score.** Eight weighted factors — market, timing, moat, unit economics, execution, science, legal, competition — grounded in an 18-sector knowledge base. It's deterministic: the same input always produces the same number, and every factor shows its weight and rationale. Not a black box.

→ **A four-role expert council.** A scientist, a data analyst, an economist, and a lawyer each write their view and flag risks, then a synthesis memo lands on a verdict: invest, watch, or pass.

→ **A concrete entry strategy.** Lead ticket and range, target ownership, pre-money valuation band, staged tranches by milestone, risk-adjusted MOIC and target IRR, and portfolio sizing via fractional-Kelly.

You can export the memo to PDF, compare two deals side by side (with a factor-by-factor delta), save deals to a watchlist, and share a public report — so a scout can turn a founder chat into a fund-grade memo in one click.

It's built for angels, micro-VCs, scouts, and syndicates who want a repeatable rubric instead of gut feel. It's a screening tool to make judgment faster and more transparent — not investment advice.
