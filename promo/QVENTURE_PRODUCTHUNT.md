## First comment (the maker's intro — post immediately after launch)
```
Hi Product Hunt 👋

Most "AI for investors" tools give you a fuzzy score you can't argue with. I wanted
the opposite — something a partner could defend in an IC meeting.

Paste any company description (any sector) and QVenture returns three things in ~30s:

• A deterministic 0–100 score across 8 weighted factors (market, timing, moat, unit
  economics, execution, science, legal, competition), each grounded in an 18-sector
  knowledge base with cited market sizes. Same input → same number. The scoring is
  plain code, not an LLM, so every point is auditable.

• A 4-role AI council — scientist, data analyst, economist, lawyer — that each write
  a view and flag risk, then a synthesis memo lands on invest / watch / pass.

• A concrete entry strategy: ticket, valuation band, tranches, risk-adjusted MOIC/IRR,
  fractional-Kelly sizing.

New: a benchmark signal — every analysis is stored, so a new deal gets a real
percentile against every deal QVenture has scored. It sharpens as the tool is used.

It's a screening aid, explicitly not investment advice. Free to try, no signup:
https://aevion.vercel.app/qventure  ·  see examples across sectors:
https://aevion.vercel.app/qventure/gallery

Would genuinely love feedback on the factor weights from anyone who runs diligence —
that's the part I most want to get right. Happy to answer anything!
```
